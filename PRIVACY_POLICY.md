# Eat Something Privacy Policy

Eat Something helps you pick a dinner option and search Apple Maps for nearby restaurants.

## Data We Store

The app stores your dinner options, reminder settings, spin count, and review prompt state locally on your device using Apple system storage. This information is not sent to us.

## Location

Eat Something only requests your location when you tap the location button on the restaurant search screen. Your location is used to open an Apple Maps search near you. The app does not store your location or send it to our own servers.

## Notifications

If you enable weekly reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. These reminders are managed by iOS and are not sent through our servers.

## Third Parties

The app can open Apple Maps to search for restaurants. Apple Maps is provided by Apple and is subject to Apple's privacy practices.

The app does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or third-party tracking.
